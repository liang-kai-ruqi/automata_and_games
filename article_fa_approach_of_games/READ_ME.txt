This folder contains the related programs and running results for the paper "A Finite Automaton Approach to Combinatorial Game".
arxiv link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13273

Under the directory:
- original: 
  The original program of the paper. There are some known issues, such as: the results contain redundant parts causing the txt files too large； contain non ASCII characters; the proof conditions of Kotzig's nim SG automata are incorrect. These issues do not affect the main results of the original paper， and will be fixed in subsequent updates.
- octal_numbers:
  A paper generated by AI that provides a series of counterexamples to Problem 3.1 in the original paper. The structure of these counterexamples is not complicated, but they're not noticed when writing the paper. These counterexamples mean that the conjecture in Problem 3.1. does not hold, "admit an outcome DFA" should be changed to a weaker property, such as "the outcome series of single heaps are ultimately periodic".
